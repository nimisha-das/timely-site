FAQ

Honest answers, kept short.

How is this different from a to-do list?

A to-do list keeps adding up. Timely bundles to-dos under their projects, shows each project’s health, and puts one small thing on today.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up, no analytics and no tracking.

Where is my data?

In your browser, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Export a backup file any time and restore it whenever you need to.

What does Google Calendar do?

It’s optional and one-way. Only to-dos you mark In Calendar are added, and Timely never reads your other events. See the privacy policy.

Does it only work in Chrome?

Yes. Timely lives in Chrome’s side panel.
